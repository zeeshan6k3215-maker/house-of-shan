THE ORIGIN

Every house has a founder. Ours has a name.

Shan means pride, dignity, grandeur — and it is the name this house was built to honour. The monogram folds two initials into one mark: a sharp, angular S and a looping, deliberate Z, carved together the way a signature is carved into wax.

"A fragrance should announce a name before it announces a note."
